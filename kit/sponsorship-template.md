# UNPAID SPONSORSHIP (20–35 seconds, vertical)

Klaudia makes an ad nobody asked for and nobody paid for, for a product, app or company she likes. Deadpan, proud, a little too serious. The joke: it's a real ad in every way except that nobody paid her. She wins anyway.

Shape (fill the blanks):
```
[Intro - dry deadpan spoken woman, almost no music]
This video is not sponsored by <BRAND>.
<a cheeky second line: "They don't know I exist." / "Nobody paid me. I paid myself.">

[Verse - cold deadpan spoken, fast, beat drop, choir shouts it back]
<true thing 1 about the product, max 7 words>
(<SHOUTED ECHO>!)
<true thing 2>
(<SHOUTED ECHO>!)
<what she uses it for, absurd but harmless>
(<ONE WORD>!)

[Chorus - shouted, call and response]
<BRAND>, <2–3 words>!
(<ECHO>! <ECHO>!)
Unpaid! Unasked!
(<ECHO>! <ECHO>!)

[Outro - the music cuts dead, dry spoken woman]
<BRAND>, call me.
<"___ yourself." sign-off>

[End]
```
Rules: only true things about the product (from its own site or news) · the name in words, never its logo, packaging copy or app screens · on screen and in the post: "unpaid · not affiliated with <BRAND>" · no medicine, alcohol, gambling, weapons, adult products, financial products, coins or tokens · never mock a real person who works there.
Pictures: Klaudia using or showing the product in her world (generic objects, no brand marks), a big "hero" shot of it, the robots reacting. 6–8 shots.
