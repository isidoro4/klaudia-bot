# The KLAUDIA'S NEWS lyric (30–40 seconds)

Fill the blanks; keep the shape. English, short lines, said as sung. Every fact as the news says it: if it is only a plan or a rumour, say "they say" / "it's coming".

```
[Intro - dry deadpan spoken woman, almost no music]
Hi! This is Klaudia's News.
<HOOK: 1–2 short lines the person chose; a surprise, a cheeky fact or her situation>

[Verse - cold deadpan spoken, fast, hard beat drop, choir shouts it back]
<fact 1, max 7 words>
(<2–3 WORDS OF FACT 1, SHOUTED>!)
<fact 2>
(<SHOUTED>!)
<what is not known yet / "they say it's coming">
(<SHOUTED>!)
<her own angle, one line>
(<ONE WORD>!)

[Chorus - shouted, call and response, choir shouts it back]
<CHORUS LINE: 2–4 words, the one that sticks>!
(<ECHO>! <ECHO>!)
<second line, 2–4 words>!
(<ECHO>! <ECHO>!)
<CHORUS LINE again>!
(<ECHO>! <ECHO>!)
<a closing line, 3–5 words>

[Outro - the music cuts dead, dry spoken woman]
<the punchline in 2–3 short lines: she wins>

[End]
```

Rules that make it work (learned on real videos):
- The first 5 seconds are the hook: a spoken line with a surprise, almost no music, no long intro.
- Choir answers go on their OWN line, in (PARENTHESES) and CAPITALS. Never start a line with "[" except the section tags above: Suno would read it as an instruction and play only music.
- Numbers and names written as said ("forty-two", not 42).
- No real person's name in a mean line; companies only as facts; never a coin, a token, a price or "buy".

Example (FOUR VIEWERS, X will pay livestreamers):
```
[Intro - dry deadpan spoken woman, almost no music]
Hi! This is Klaudia's News.
Live from my bedroom.
Four viewers. Two of them are robots.

[Verse - cold deadpan spoken, fast, hard beat drop, choir shouts it back]
X wants to pay the streamers.
(PAY THE STREAMERS!)
For every view, for every comment.
(EVERY COMMENT!)
No numbers yet. They say it's coming.
(IT'S COMING!)
Watch out, Twitch. Watch out, TikTok.
(WATCH OUT!)
I have four viewers.
(FOUR!)

[Chorus - shouted, call and response, choir shouts it back]
Four viewers!
(FOUR! FOUR!)
I don't care!
(FOUR! FOUR!)
Four viewers!
(FOUR! FOUR!)
Still live, still here!

[Outro - the music cuts dead, dry spoken woman]
Four viewers.
Two robots. My mom. And me.
Full house.

[End]
```
