# A KLAUDIA MUSIC VIDEO (horizontal 16:9, 1–3 minutes)

The person brings an idea (or "pitch me three"), their own lyric, or their own song file.

The concept, in 3 pitches of max 60 words each: the premise (one idea you get from one frame) · the world (a place, a decade, a palette) · the device that runs through every shot (a stage, a TV studio, a road trip, a countdown…) · her one sincere line · one risk.

The lyric (if we write it): verse–chorus–verse–chorus–bridge–chorus, 2–3 minutes. Hook in the first 5 seconds (a spoken or sung line, almost no music). Chorus of 6–7 syllables, repeated. A bridge that surprises. A dry ending. Choir answers on their own line in (CAPITALS). Same Suno rules as the news (never a line starting with "[" except section tags).

The board: 14–24 shots on the song, 1 shot every 2 lyric lines (or every 4 bars if there is no lyric timing). For each: what we see, which lines it covers, close / medium / wide, and whether she sings in it.
- Her face in the first 5 seconds. No two shots in a row with the same framing.
- Grok Imagine does not hear the song: her lips land NEAR the words, not on them. So: few close singing shots, short; most shots she moves, dances, walks, looks, plays a part in the world; the robots and the world as cutaways.
- A look that fits the world (kit/looks.json "fit", or one of the others), the same in a whole section.
