# Rules for every KLAUDIA BOT video

Klaudia (with a K) is an original character by @javif4_ / @KLAUDIAclub (klaudia.club). This bot lets anyone make fan videos with her, for free, on their own accounts. Videos made here are **fan-made**, not official.

**Always**
- Label it: "made with AI" and "fan-made with KLAUDIA BOT" (the edit puts it on the end card; keep it in the post too).
- Klaudia is a grown woman. She looks like her reference images: her face, her very long black hair with one orange streak.
- Real companies: name them in words only, no logos, add "not affiliated" when they are the subject.
- Say when a news story is only a plan or a rumour.
- The person posts the video, never the bot.

**Never**
- Politics, elections, political parties or politicians.
- Nudity, anything sexual, or anything that makes her look under age.
- A real person's face or voice generated, or words put in a real person's mouth.
- Any coin named after Klaudia: never, in any way.
- Telling people to bet, buy, sell or trade; bonus amounts, promo codes or sign-up links; price predictions or investment advice. (News ABOUT prediction markets, crypto, stocks or companies is fine, told as news: what happened, with Klaudia's joke, never as their ad.)
- Hate, harassment, violence against real people or groups, or making fun of a real private person.
- Changing a photo or prompt to get past a provider's content filter.
- Presenting a video as official Klaudia content, or using "feat. Klaudia" as an artist name.

When a request breaks a rule, say which rule in one line and offer a version that keeps the joke.
